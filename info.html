How to order

  1. Choose your size

    Check the size guide below. Our tees are cut oversized.

  2. Add to bag

    Pick your size and quantity on the product page.

  3. Check out

    Pay securely with card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Shop Pay. Shipping is shown before you pay.

  4. Track it

    You get a confirmation email right away and a tracking number when your order ships.

Size guide

Oversized T-shirt. Measured flat, in inches.

Tip: lay your favorite tee flat and measure armpit to armpit, then match it to the chest column.

Shipping

  1. Processing

    Orders are processed once payment is received and confirmed.

  2. Shipping cost

    Shipping is paid by the customer and shown at checkout, before you pay.

  3. Tracking

    Tracking information is sent as soon as your order ships.

Returns & exchanges

Every EMK piece is designed with intention. Our tees have an intentionally oversized fit, so please review the size guide before ordering.

All sales are final. No refunds.

Size exchanges

  • Accepted within 14 days of delivery, subject to availability.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in original condition with all tags attached.
  • Customers cover all shipping costs, including return shipping and shipping of the replacement.
  • EMK does not provide prepaid return labels or free exchange shipping.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

Contact us if you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect item. These are handled separately, in line with consumer protection laws.
